SAMIR: Hard to say. I've assumed it holds flat for now.

GEORGES: And the warehouse expansion? I need to know whether I'm planning for it.

GEORGES: Fine. Q3, understood.

RANA: Next, the shipping vendor. We had two proposals. Lina, you reviewed both?

RANA: So we go with Delta Freight. Everyone agree? Good — Delta Freight it is. We rejected Caspian on the twelve-day transit time, let's make sure that's in the minutes so nobody reopens it in March.

SAMIR: Noted.

RANA: Lina, the tracking system. Where are we?

LINA: We need to migrate off the old platform. I'll own the tracking system migration.

RANA: Good. When?

LINA: Give me until the fourteenth of March. That's realistic, anything earlier is not.

RANA: Fourteenth of March, you own it. Georges, headcount?

GEORGES: I need drivers. We're turning down routes.

RANA: How many?

GEORGES: Five would be comfortable.

SAMIR: Five isn't in the budget.

RANA: Three. We're approving three new drivers for this quarter, and we revisit in Q2 if the route volume holds.

GEORGES: I'll take three.

RANA: Last item — insurance renewal is coming up. Samir, that's yours.

SAMIR: I'll handle the insurance renewal, yes.

RANA: Good. Let's put the next review on the second of February. Same room. Thanks everyone.
