MEETING 02 — Hiring Round
Meridian Logistics · 2 February 2026 · Conference Room B
Language: English
Attendees: Rana Khalil (Operations Director), Samir Haddad (Finance), Lina Aoun (IT), Georges Nassar (Warehouse)

---

RANA: Okay, hiring. Georges, drivers first, because I know that's what you came for.

GEORGES: It is. So we posted the driver ad on the twentieth, and we got forty-seven applications. Forty-seven. More than I expected.

SAMIR: How many were actually qualified?

GEORGES: Honestly? Maybe a dozen with the right licence. We interviewed eight, and —

RANA: And?

GEORGES: — and we've signed two of them. Two contracts, done, signed on Friday.

RANA: Only two? I thought we —

GEORGES: The third one backed out on Thursday. Took a job with a competitor. So we're still short of what you approved last month.

RANA: Okay. Keep the search open. What are we paying them?

SAMIR: Starting salary is fourteen hundred dollars a month. That's what's in the budget, I'm not moving on it.

GEORGES: Samir, the competitor offered more, that's exactly why he —

SAMIR: Fourteen hundred. I'm not reopening the budget in February.

RANA: Fourteen hundred stays. But Georges, why do we keep losing people? Is it just pay?

GEORGES: Partly. Our driver turnover last year was twenty-two percent. Twenty-two. Every fifth driver walked.

LINA: That's — wow. That's high.

RANA: So what fixes it? Not money, apparently.

GEORGES: Referrals. People who come in through a friend stay longer, every time.

RANA: Fine. Let's do a referral bonus. Samir, what can we afford?

SAMIR: Three hundred dollars per referral, paid once the driver is hired. Not before.

RANA: Three hundred dollars, paid on hire. Done. Georges, when do the two new drivers start?

GEORGES: Monday the sixteenth of February.

RANA: And who's onboarding them? Because last time —

GEORGES: I know, I know, last time it was a mess. I'll run the onboarding myself. I'll own it.

RANA: Good. Lina, you wanted someone for IT.

LINA: Yes. I need IT support help for the migration. I can't run the tracking cutover and answer every printer ticket at the same time.

SAMIR: Full-time?

LINA: I was going to ask for full-time, but —

RANA: No. Let's make it a contractor. Three months.

LINA: Why not permanent?

RANA: Because we only need the extra hands until the tracking migration is finished. After that we'd be paying a permanent salary for a job that doesn't exist.

LINA: That's fair. Okay, a three-month contractor.

SAMIR: And cap it. Nine thousand dollars total for the contract, all in.

LINA: Nine thousand for three months? That's tight, but — okay. I can find someone at that.

RANA: Nine thousand cap. Anything else? No? Good. Thanks everyone.
